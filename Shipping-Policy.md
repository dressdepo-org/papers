
# Shipping Policy

**Last Updated: 13 November 2026**

Welcome to **DressDepo** ([https://dressdepo.com](https://dressdepo.com)). This Shipping Policy outlines how we process, ship, track, and deliver orders placed through our platform.

## 1. Shipping Coverage

DressDepo provides shipping services to eligible PIN codes across India.

Delivery availability depends on the customer's location and courier serviceability and is confirmed during checkout.

## 2. Order Processing Time

Orders are generally processed within **1–3 business days** after order confirmation.

Processing includes order verification, packaging, and preparation for dispatch.

## 3. Estimated Delivery Time

Once an order has been dispatched, the estimated delivery time is **5–9 business days**, depending on the delivery location and courier availability.

The total estimated time from order confirmation to delivery is generally **6–12 business days**.

Estimated delivery dates are displayed during checkout where available.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to circumstances beyond our control.

## 4. Shipping Charges

**DressDepo offers free standard shipping on all eligible orders within India.**

- No additional standard shipping or delivery charges are applied.
- Shipping charges are not added separately to the product price at checkout.
- Any applicable Cash on Delivery (COD) handling fees are displayed separately before order confirmation.

Customers can review the complete payable amount before placing their orders.

## 5. Shipping Partners

DressDepo works with third-party courier and logistics providers to fulfill customer orders.

Products may be shipped from different sellers or fulfillment locations across India.

Courier assignment depends on serviceability, shipment characteristics, and delivery location.

## 6. Order Tracking

Once an order has been dispatched, tracking information is made available through the customer's DressDepo account.

To track an order:

1. Log in to your DressDepo account.
2. Navigate to **Orders**.
3. Click on **View Order** button on the relevant order.
4. Click on **🚚 Track Order** button.

Tracking updates depend on the courier partner and may not always be available immediately after dispatch.

## 7. Delayed or Failed Deliveries

Delivery delays may occur due to circumstances such as:

- Weather conditions or natural disasters.
- Courier or transportation disruptions.
- Incorrect or incomplete delivery addresses.
- Customer unavailability during delivery attempts.
- Regional restrictions or other unforeseen circumstances.

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, the courier may attempt redelivery or return the shipment to its origin (RTO), depending on the courier's procedures and delivery circumstances.

Customers may contact DressDepo support for assistance with delayed or unsuccessful deliveries.

## 8. Returns and Refunds

Eligible products may be returned within **5 days of successful delivery**, subject to DressDepo's Return and Refund Policy.

Customers can initiate an eligible return through their DressDepo account.

For detailed information about return eligibility, refund procedures, and applicable conditions, please refer to our **Return and Refund Policy**.
