# DressDepo Return & Refund Policy

## Return Policy

DressDepo accepts returns within **5 days of successful delivery** for eligible products.

Customers can return products for reasons such as incorrect size, fit issues, damaged products, wrong products, quality issues, or simply changing their mind.

Products must be returned with their original tags, packaging, and accessories, where applicable. Returns are subject to verification.

## How to Return a Product

1. Open [dressdepo.com](https://dressdepo.com).
2. Log in to your account.
3. Click the **three dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **📦 Order**.
5. Find your order in the list.
6. Click the **More Details** button.
7. Click **Return**.
8. Select your **Return Reason**.
9. Click the **Return** button to submit your request.

## Refund for Prepaid Orders

For prepaid orders, the refund will be sent to your original payment method.

You will receive the amount payable after DressDepo coupons or discounts are applied. Any additional discount provided separately by the payment gateway is not included in the refund calculation.

**Example:**

- Product price: ₹500
- DressDepo coupon (10%): −₹50
- Amount after DressDepo coupon: ₹450
- Additional payment gateway discount (5%): −₹22.50
- Final amount paid: ₹427.50
- **Refund amount: ₹450**

## Refund for Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

For COD orders, you will receive a **Cashfree Cashgram refund link**, which opens in your browser.

You can claim your refund using the available payout options:

- Bank account
- UPI
- Paytm Wallet
- Amazon Pay Wallet

Available payout methods depend on Cashfree's supported options.

**Example:**

- Product price: ₹500
- DressDepo coupon (10%): −₹50
- Amount paid on delivery: ₹450
- **Refund amount: ₹450**

## Refund Conditions

- Return requests must be submitted within 5 days of successful delivery.
- Refunds are processed after the returned product is received and verified, where applicable.
- No additional return, refund, or processing fees are charged by DressDepo.
- Returns requested after 5 days are generally not accepted, except where required by applicable consumer protection laws.
- Refunds are based on the eligible order amount after DressDepo discounts.
- Cashfree Cashgram claims must be completed using the refund link provided.
