
# Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated: 13 November 2026**

Welcome to **DressDepo** ([https://dressdepo.com](https://dressdepo.com)).

At DressDepo, we are committed to providing a transparent, convenient, and reliable shopping experience. We understand that customers may occasionally need to return a product, cancel an order, or request a refund.

This Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the eligibility requirements, procedures, refund methods, and timelines applicable to orders placed on DressDepo.

This policy applies to eligible orders placed through our platform within India.

---

## 1. Return Eligibility

DressDepo allows customers to request a return within **5 calendar days from the date of successful delivery**.

Customers may request returns for reasons including:

- Incorrect size or fit.
- Product not meeting expectations.
- Damaged or defective products.
- Incorrect product received.
- Product significantly different from its description.
- Other valid return reasons available on the platform.

### Product Condition Requirements

For standard returns, the following conditions apply:

1. The product must be in good and original condition.
2. The product must not have been used, washed, altered, or damaged by the customer.
3. Original tags, labels, and packaging should be retained wherever applicable.
4. All accessories and components originally included with the product must be returned.
5. The customer must submit the return request within the applicable return window.

Products that fail to meet these requirements may be subject to additional inspection, refund adjustments, or return rejection, where justified and legally permitted.

**Important:** Products received damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from their descriptions will be handled appropriately under applicable consumer protection laws. Standard condition requirements will not be used to unfairly deny legitimate claims.

## 2. How to Request a Return

Customers can request returns directly through their DressDepo account.

The return request procedure depends on the original payment method.

### 2.1 Return Process for Prepaid Orders

For orders paid online using UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, or other supported payment methods:

1. Log in to your DressDepo account.
2. Navigate to **Orders**.
3. Find the order you wish to return.
4. Click **More Details**.
5. Click the **Return** button.
6. Select the appropriate **return reason**.
7. Submit your request.
8. Your return request will be successfully registered.

**No additional UPI ID or bank account details are required for prepaid returns.**

After the returned product reaches the seller and the refund is approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method used during checkout.

### 2.2 Return Process for Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

For orders paid through Cash on Delivery:

1. Log in to your DressDepo account.
2. Navigate to **Orders**.
3. Find the order you wish to return.
4. Click **More Details**.
5. Click the **Return** button.
6. Select the appropriate **return reason**.
7. Enter your **correct and active UPI ID** in the provided field.
8. Click the **Done** button.
9. Your return request will be successfully registered.

**Important:** COD refunds are currently supported through UPI transfers only.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that the UPI ID provided is accurate and capable of receiving payments.

Incorrect UPI details may result in failed transfers or refund delays.

## 3. Return Shipping and Pickup

DressDepo provides **free return shipping for eligible returns**.

After a return request is submitted, DressDepo or its courier partners will provide the applicable return instructions.

Where reverse pickup is available, the product may be collected from the customer's eligible pickup address.

If reverse pickup is unavailable, customers will be informed about available alternatives.

Customers should securely package the returned product to prevent damage during transportation.

No separate return shipping fee will be charged for an eligible return arranged through DressDepo.

## 4. Return Delivery and Seller Inspection

Once the returned product is successfully delivered back to the seller, the seller will inspect its condition before approving the refund.

The inspection may include verification of:

- Product condition and appearance.
- Original tags and labels.
- Signs of use, washing, or alteration.
- Physical damage.
- Completeness of the returned item.
- Whether the product matches the original order.

### 4.1 Product Received in Good Condition

If the product is received in good and original condition and meets the return requirements:

- The seller will approve the return.
- The seller will release the refund for the **full eligible amount paid by the customer for the returned product**.
- DressDepo will initiate the refund using the applicable payment method.
- The customer will receive a refund confirmation email.

### 4.2 Product Not Received in Good Condition

If the returned product is found to be used, washed, altered, damaged by the customer, incomplete, or otherwise not in its original acceptable condition, the seller may temporarily hold the refund while reviewing the issue.

In such circumstances:

1. DressDepo or the seller will contact the customer using their **registered email address** and/or the **phone number provided in the delivery address**.
2. The customer will be informed about the inspection findings and the reason for any refund hold.
3. Additional information or clarification may be requested.
4. Where justified and legally permitted, the seller may propose a reduced refund amount or reject a standard return.
5. The customer will be informed of the decision and the reason for any deduction.

Any refund deduction must be based on the actual issue identified and must not be arbitrary or unfair.

Customers may contact DressDepo to dispute the inspection findings or proposed refund amount.

Customer rights concerning manufacturing defects, incorrect products, delivery damage, or misdescribed goods remain protected under applicable Indian laws.

## 5. Refund Methods

DressDepo processes approved refunds according to the original order payment method.

### 5.1 Refunds for Prepaid Orders

For prepaid orders, refunds will be processed to the **original payment method used during checkout**.

| Original Payment Method | Refund Destination |
| --- | --- |
| UPI | Original UPI payment source |
| Debit Card | Original debit card |
| Credit Card | Original credit card |
| Net Banking | Original payment source |
| Other supported prepaid methods | Original payment method, where supported |

Customers do not need to provide a separate UPI ID or bank account for standard prepaid refunds.

For returned products, the refund will be initiated after the seller receives, inspects, and approves the return.

### 5.2 Refunds for Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

For COD orders, approved refunds are currently processed **through UPI only**.

Customers must provide a valid UPI ID while submitting their return request.

COD refunds are currently processed manually.

Once the seller approves and releases the refund, DressDepo will arrange the transfer to the customer's submitted UPI ID.

Customers should ensure that their UPI details are correct to avoid unnecessary delays.

## 6. Refund Amount

For an approved full-order refund, DressDepo will refund the **full eligible amount actually paid by the customer for the order**.

For a return involving only selected products, the refund amount will be calculated based on the amount attributable to those products, including applicable discounts and refundable charges.

Where DressDepo promotional coupons, third-party payment discounts, or other offers were applied, the refund will be calculated according to the amount actually paid, the applicable offer terms, and the customer's legal refund entitlement.

Any proposed refund deduction due to the condition of a returned product will be communicated to the customer with an explanation.

There are no additional return-shipping or refund-processing charges for standard eligible returns arranged through DressDepo.

## 7. Refund Confirmation Email

Once the seller releases the refund, DressDepo will send a **refund confirmation email to the customer's registered email address**.

Receiving a refund confirmation email means that the refund has been released or initiated for processing.

The actual time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the relevant bank, UPI network, card issuer, or payment provider.

## 8. Order Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel eligible orders directly through their DressDepo account while the cancellation option remains available.

### 8.1 How to Cancel an Order

1. Log in to your DressDepo account.
2. Navigate to **Orders**.
3. Select the order you wish to cancel.
4. Open **More Details**.
5. Select the **cancellation reason**.
6. Click the **Cancel Order** button.
7. The eligible order will be cancelled and its cancellation status updated.

Order cancellation is available only while the order is eligible for cancellation, generally before dispatch.

Once an order has been dispatched, the cancellation option may no longer be available. Customers may instead request a return after successful delivery, subject to the return eligibility requirements.

### 8.2 Immediate Refund for Cancelled Prepaid Orders

For eligible prepaid orders, **the refund will be initiated immediately after the customer successfully cancels the order by clicking the Cancel Order button**.

- No seller inspection is required for an order cancelled before dispatch.
- No return shipment is required.
- The refund will be initiated automatically to the original payment method.
- The full eligible amount paid for the cancelled order will be refunded.
- No additional UPI ID or bank account details are required.

**Important:** Immediate refund initiation does not necessarily mean that the money will appear in the customer's bank account immediately.

The final credit time depends on the bank, card issuer, or payment provider.

### 8.3 Cancellation of COD Orders

For eligible COD orders cancelled before dispatch:

- The order will be cancelled successfully.
- No refund will ordinarily be required because no payment has been collected.
- No UPI ID is required for the cancellation.

### 8.4 Cancellation by DressDepo or the Seller

DressDepo or the seller may cancel an order because of product unavailability, payment verification issues, operational limitations, or other legitimate reasons.

If an already-paid order is cancelled, DressDepo will arrange a refund of the applicable paid amount to the original payment method.

Customers will be informed of the cancellation through the available communication channels.

## 9. Refund Processing Timelines

Refund processing depends on the type of request.

### 9.1 Cancelled Prepaid Orders

For successful customer-initiated cancellations of eligible prepaid orders, the refund will be **initiated immediately upon cancellation**.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the original payment method depends on the applicable payment provider and banking processes.

### 9.2 Returned Products

For returns, refund processing begins after the returned product has been successfully received by the seller and the required inspection and approval have been completed.

If the seller has neither released the refund nor contacted the customer within **7 business days after receiving the returned product**, the customer may escalate the matter to DressDepo.

This seven-business-day period is an escalation threshold, not an authorization to delay refunds beyond any deadline required by applicable law.

### 9.3 Prepaid RTO Orders

For prepaid orders marked as Return to Origin (RTO) due to customer unavailability or unsuccessful delivery attempts, the refund will be processed after the failed delivery or RTO status has been confirmed.

**If the customer does not receive their refund within 2–3 business days after the order is confirmed as RTO, they are requested to contact DressDepo by email for assistance.**

The refund will be processed to the original payment method used during checkout.

Actual credit times may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.

### 9.4 Business Days

For the purpose of refund and escalation timelines, business days generally mean Monday to Friday, excluding applicable public and banking holidays.

## 12. Partial Returns and Refunds

If an order contains multiple products, customers may request returns for individual eligible products.

The refund amount will be calculated based on the eligible amount paid for the products being returned.

Any order-level discounts, promotional offers, or refundable charges will be allocated appropriately when calculating partial refunds.

The approved refund amount will be communicated to the customer.

For prepaid orders, the amount will be refunded through the original payment method.

For COD orders, the amount will be refunded to the customer's provided UPI ID.

## 13. Incorrect, Damaged, or Defective Products

If a customer receives an incorrect, damaged, defective, or materially misdescribed product, they should submit a return request as soon as possible through their DressDepo account.

The customer may be asked to provide supporting photographs or other reasonable information to help investigate the issue.

DressDepo and the seller will review the request and arrange an appropriate resolution.

Customers will not be subjected to unfair refund deductions for defects, damage, or incorrect items for which they are not responsible.

Nothing in this policy restricts rights or remedies provided by applicable consumer protection laws.
