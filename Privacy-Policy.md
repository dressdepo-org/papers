This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you use DressDepo’s website, customer accounts, seller portal, and warehouse services.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we collect:

- **Account details:** Name, email address, sign-in method, password hash for password-based accounts, and avatar preferences.
- **Google sign-in details:** Your name and email address when you choose Google sign-in.
- **Delivery details:** Recipient name, telephone number, delivery address, city, district, state, and postal code.
- **Shopping activity:** Cart items, wishlists, product selections, sizes, quantities, search queries, search history, and coupon usage.
- **Order information:** Purchased items, order amounts, payment methods, transaction references, payment status, shipping information, cancellations, returns, and refunds.
- **Reviews:** Ratings, comments, and review likes or dislikes.
- **Seller and warehouse details:** Contact information, store names, logos, support details, warehouse addresses, pickup information, vendor names, and GST identification numbers where provided.
- **Technical information:** Authentication information and request or error logs generated while operating our services.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Manage accounts and authenticate users.
- Save shopping preferences, carts, wishlists, and addresses.
- Process orders and arrange deliveries.
- Manage returns, cancellations, and refunds.
- Send verification codes and service communications.
- Display reviews and operate seller and warehouse tools.
- Improve product search and maintain our services.
- Investigate misuse, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Some information is necessary to provide a service. For example, we need your delivery address and telephone number to arrange shipping.

## 3. Product Search

DressDepo uses an AI service to match search queries with relevant products. Search text and product information may be sent to the configured AI service provider for this purpose.

Please avoid entering passwords, payment credentials, or sensitive personal information into the search box.

## 4. Sharing Information

We share relevant information with:

- **Sellers and authorised warehouse or packing staff** who fulfil orders.
- **Shipping providers**, including Shiprocket and delivery partners.
- **Payment service providers**, where needed to process transactions and refunds.
- **Email providers**, including Resend, for verification and service communications.
- **Google Cloud Platform and other infrastructure providers** needed to operate our services.
- **AI service providers** used for product search.
- **Authorities or professional advisers**, where required by law or necessary to resolve disputes and protect legal rights.

Third-party services handle information under their own privacy policies and service arrangements.

## 6. Public Information

Reviews, ratings, display names, and avatars may be publicly visible. Published seller store details, logos, product listings, and support information may also be visible to other users.

Do not include private or sensitive information in public reviews.

## 7. Cookies and Browser Storage

We use cookies and browser storage to maintain sign-in sessions and remember preferences, including preferences within seller tools.

You can clear or restrict these through your browser settings. This may affect sign-in and other features.

## 8. Data Retention

We generally retain account and service records indefinitely unless they are deleted following a valid request or their deletion is required by applicable law.

Certain transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, legal obligations, fraud prevention, or unresolved disputes.

You can delete saved search history through the available account controls.

## 9. Security

The application uses measures such as password hashing and token-based authentication to help protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

## 10. Your Choices and Requests

You can update available profile settings, manage saved addresses, and delete saved search history.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. We may verify your identity before handling a request.

Requests are subject to applicable law and any necessary recordkeeping obligations.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

DressDepo does not currently specify a minimum age for browsing its website.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent to process a child’s personal information, that consent must be obtained. A parent or guardian may contact us with concerns or requests concerning their child’s information.
